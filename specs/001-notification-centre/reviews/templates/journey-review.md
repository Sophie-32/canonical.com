## Candidate findings

For each observation, record a stable finding ID, reproducible steps, persona,
expected/actual behaviour, evidence, severity and task impact. Label self-reported
confusion and unverified suggestions separately from observed failure. "None"
is a valid result. A tool limitation is not automatically a product defect.
